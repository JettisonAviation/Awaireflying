This Privacy Policy describes how Awaire Technologies LLC ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Airtura mobile application and related services (together, the "Service").

"Airtura" is our brand name; the company providing the Service is Awaire Technologies LLC, a California limited liability company.

By using the Service, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

01
Who We Are

Awaire Technologies LLC operates the Airtura flight-intelligence product. Airtura provides flight intelligence to passengers, including real-time flight status, delay explanations, turbulence forecasts, and plain-language context about what is happening with a flight.

Contact: legal@awaireapp.com

02
Information We Collect
Information You Provide Directly
  • Account information. When you create an account, we collect your email address and a password. Passwords are hashed and we never see them in plain text. If you sign in with Apple, we receive only the limited identity information Apple shares with us (which may include your name and a relay or actual email address). If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google.
  • Flight searches. We record the flight numbers and dates you look up so we can show you your tracked flights and relevant notifications.
  • Saved and tracked flights. Flights you choose to save or track are stored on your account.
  • Feedback you submit. When you submit feedback through the in-app feedback form, we record your message, your selected category (bug, feature, general), and your email address so we can follow up.
  • Communications. If you email us, we keep a record of that correspondence.
Information Collected Automatically
  • Device and technical information. Basic data needed to deliver the Service, such as device type, operating system, app version, IP address, and general location derived from IP.
  • Push notification tokens. If you grant notification permission, we store an Apple Push Notification service (APNs) token so we can send you flight updates.
  • Session data. Authentication session tokens are stored on your device to keep you signed in.
  • Subscription and purchase data. When you purchase a subscription or Tura Pass through the App Store, Apple processes your payment directly — we never see or store your credit card, debit card, or other payment-method details. We do receive and store information about your subscription state (such as which product you purchased, whether it is active, and when it renews or expires) through our subscription processor, RevenueCat, so we can grant you access to paid features and restore your purchases across devices.
Information We Do NOT Collect
  • We do not use third-party analytics, advertising trackers, Mixpanel, PostHog, Google Analytics, or similar tools.
  • We do not sell or "share" your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites (we do not engage in tracking governed by Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework).

04
AI-Generated Content

The Service uses generative artificial intelligence (AI) to produce significant portions of the content shown in the app, including (without limitation) flight briefings, "Captain AI" chat replies, delay explanations, post-flight summaries, and other text content. To generate this content, we send flight context (flight number, route, current status, weather conditions, and delay information) to Anthropic, the operator of the Claude AI model.

AI-generated content can be inaccurate, incomplete, biased, or out-of-date. The Service is informational only and is not a substitute for official flight or safety information from the airline or flight crew.

How We Share Information

We share information only with the following third parties, each of which plays a necessary role in operating the Service:

Supabase
Stores your account, authentication sessions, flight searches, saved flights, and feedback submissions. supabase.com/privacy
Apple
Processes your login if you sign in with Apple, and delivers push notifications to your device through Apple Push Notification service. apple.com/legal/privacy
Google
Processes your login if you sign in with Google. policies.google.com/privacy
Railway
Hosts the backend servers that retrieve and process your flight data. railway.com/legal/privacy
AeroAPI (FlightAware), AVWX, FAA NASSTATUS, and other aviation-data providers
We send flight numbers and dates to these providers to retrieve current flight status, weather, and operational data.
Anthropic
We send flight context to Anthropic's Claude model to generate AI briefings, chat replies, and delay explanations. anthropic.com/legal/privacy
Apple In-App Purchase
Apple processes all subscription and Tura Pass payments and handles billing, renewal, refunds, and family-sharing entitlement directly. We do not see or store your payment-method details. apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes
RevenueCat
Manages our subscription state on our behalf, including which products you have purchased, whether your subscription is active, when it renews or expires, and entitlement status across your devices. RevenueCat receives a pseudonymous user identifier, device identifiers, and Apple App Store transaction events. revenuecat.com/privacy

We may also disclose information when required by law, subpoena, or legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Awaire Technologies LLC, our users, or others.

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, user information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active.

You may delete your account at any time:

  • In-app, through the account screen (in compliance with Apple App Store guideline 5.1.1(v))

After deletion, we may retain limited information for legal compliance, fraud prevention, or legitimate business record-keeping. Aviation-data provider logs (such as flight lookups) may persist with third parties under their own retention policies.
